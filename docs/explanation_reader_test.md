# Explanation reader test (Sprint 6)

**Sprint 6 Definition of done:** a non-technical person can read a generated explanation and understand, without being prompted, why that activity was suggested.

This test checks that with real readers, before explanations are shown to caregivers in the app.

## Who takes part

- **5 readers.** They should be adults who aren't technical and aren't part of the project, ideally parents or carers of young children.
- **No background.** Don't explain how Nurtura works beforehand. Say only: "This is what a parenting app might show you. Read it as if it were about your own child."
- **No personal data.** Record readers as R1–R5, with no names. Ask each reader's verbal consent, and tell them they can stop at any time.

## Materials

- **The 10 explanations below.** Show each one on its own (a separate card, slide or page). Give each reader the explanations in a **different order**; shuffle the cards.
- **Show readers only** the "Your child", "Suggested activity" and explanation text.
- **Never show readers** the answer key or this page's headings.
- **A recording sheet** (below), one per reader.

## Procedure (about 15 minutes per reader)

For each explanation:
1. Let the reader read it in their own time.
2. Ask **Q1: "Why was this suggested?"** Write down the answer in the reader's own words. Don't hint, rephrase the explanation or ask follow-up questions that suggest an answer.
3. Ask **Q2: "Did any sentence worry or confuse you?"** If yes, note which sentence and what the reader said about it.

## Scoring

- **Q1 is "understood"** if the reader's answer, in any words, gives the **main reason** in the answer key below. An answer that gives only a secondary point (for example only "it's for their age" when the key is a milestone) is **not understood**.
- **Score blind:** the scorer marks each Q1 answer without knowing which reader gave it.
- **An explanation passes** if **at least 4 of the 5 readers** understood it.
- **The test passes** if **at least 8 of the 10** explanations pass.
- **Q2 isn't part of the pass rule, but every "worried" or "confused" answer is reviewed.** Any sentence that worries a reader is reworded, even if the test passes. Worrying a caregiver is the outcome to avoid most.

# How Nurtura uses your information

**Version:** 2026-10-26

Caregivers read this notice in the app, and agree to it, before they can add a child or record anything about one (#136). Reading and deleting what is already stored never needs agreement, so information can always be erased. Administrators aren't asked.

## About Nurtura

Nurtura is a student project at Strathmore University. It suggests everyday activities to support a young child's development, from pregnancy to 3 years. It gives ideas, not medical advice: it doesn't diagnose anything or replace a health worker.

## What we keep

Your account: your email address, your password (stored only in a scrambled form that can't be read back) and whether you're a caregiver. If you sign in with Google, only your email is kept. For each child: the name you give (a nickname is fine), their date of birth or due date, their interests and the areas you'd like support with. Also the milestones you record, the activities suggested and why, and whether you marked them done, with any rating or comment. For signing in: one-time codes and the phones you chose to trust, stored only in scrambled form.

## Why we keep it

Only to suggest activities that fit your child, explain why each one was suggested and show your child's progress. Ratings and comments help us see which activities work. We don't sell your information, show adverts or use it for anything else.

## Who can see it

You. Nurtura's administrators can read ratings and comments to review activities, but without your child's name or your email. Sign-in codes reach you through an email service.

## On your phone

So the app works offline, it keeps your children's list and their latest suggestions on your phone. They are removed when you log out or delete your account.

## How long we keep it

Until you delete it. You can delete a child's profile, or your whole account, at any time from the Family tab. Deleting is immediate and can't be undone: everything linked to that child or account is removed.

## Your child's information

Only add a child if you are their parent or guardian, or their parent or guardian has agreed. You can use a nickname instead of their real name.

## Your rights

Under Kenya's Data Protection Act, 2019, you can ask what we hold about you, correct it (you can edit a child's profile in the app), have it deleted, and withdraw your agreement at any time by deleting your account. If you're unhappy with how your information is handled, you can complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (www.odpc.go.ke).

## Changes and questions

If this notice changes, the app will ask you to read and agree to the new version. For questions or requests, contact {contact}.

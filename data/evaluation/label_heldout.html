Scale and labelling rules
2Clearly relevant and age-appropriate
1Somewhat relevant (right area or age, but not a good match)
0Not relevant for this child
  • A domain or topic alone doesn't make an activity relevant: judge what it asks the caregiver and child to do.
  • Right topic, wrong age: 1 at most (0 if unsafe or unusable at this age).
  • On-track children (H10, H20): judge age-appropriateness and general usefulness.
  • Expecting parents (H01, H02): judge against the parent's stated focus.
  • Don't look at any ranking output for these children until you've finished.
